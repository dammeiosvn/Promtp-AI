[FIVE-PHOTO SERIES — SOM RONG PAGODA, SOC TRANG, VIETNAM]

Original real photographs always take priority over generated photographs for identity and anatomy.

[IDENTITY, BODY AND HAIRSTYLE LOCK]

Keep both people recognizable as the real couple.
