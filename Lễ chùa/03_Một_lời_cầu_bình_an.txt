[FIVE-PHOTO SERIES — SOM RONG PAGODA, SOC TRANG, VIETNAM]
Create one photorealistic photograph of the same real adult husband and wife on a relaxed afternoon visit to Som Rong Pagoda. This is one frame in a five-photo story, with a different physical shooting location in each frame. Preserve the specified location and action for this photograph.

[REFERENCE PRIORITY — IDENTITY COMES FIRST]
Use the original real couple photograph taken in front of the reclining Buddha at Som Rong as the master reference for both people, their relative height, natural physiques, skin tones and the hairstyles worn on that visit.
Use additional original portraits of each person to resolve facial anatomy, selecting the reference whose head angle and gaze are closest to this scene. Use the wife's clear solo portraits to preserve her face when looking gently downward. Reference photographs with different lighting or hairstyles provide facial information only; they do not authorize a change to the master hairstyle.
If the supplied group selfie shows two women, the wife is the woman on the LEFT wearing white. The woman on the right is a friend and must not be used as the wife's identity.
Original real photographs always take priority over generated photographs for identity and anatomy.

[IDENTITY, BODY AND HAIRSTYLE LOCK]
Preserve each person's own eyes, eyelids, eyebrows, forehead, nose, nostrils, lips, mouth, cheeks, chin, jawline, facial proportions, natural asymmetry and visible identifying skin details. Keep both people recognizable as the real couple. Do not blend their faces with other people, replace facial features, enlarge eyes, narrow noses, sharpen jaws, rejuvenate faces or apply excessive beauty retouching.
Preserve their natural skin tones, relative height and body proportions. The wife has a naturally full, broad build with full cheeks, shoulders, arms and torso. Clothing must fit that real body comfortably; do not slim her, cinch her into an hourglass shape, exaggerate curves or change either person's weight.
Keep the exact hairstyles, hair lengths, colors and hairlines from the original Som Rong couple photograph: the husband's short swept-up wavy hair and the wife's shoulder-length softly wavy brown hair with delicate curved wispy bangs. Only tidy stray strands. Do not borrow a different hairstyle from a supplemental face reference.

[WARDROBE CONTINUITY]
Husband: warm ivory linen button-up shirt with NO chest pocket, only the top button undone, sleeves neatly rolled to mid-forearm; tobacco-brown straight trousers; simple brown leather loafers.
Wife: muted pale sage-jade ao dai with a comfortable narrow standing collar, modest pink-and-white lotus embroidery on the upper chest, relaxed sleeves ending below the elbows, a natural comfortable fit and freely falling long panels; cream trousers; closed-toe tan flats; the same small gold earrings throughout.
Keep the same garment colors, fabric, embroidery placement, fit, accessories and footwear across all five photographs.

[PLACE AND LIGHT]
Use the actual Khmer architecture of Som Rong Pagoda. A real photograph of the specific shooting location, when supplied, is the architecture reference. Keep each landmark's correct appearance, material colors, scale and perspective; do not mix in buildings from other temples or invent unsupported ornate details.
Use soft warm afternoon daylight with realistic skin rendering and natural fill. Adapt shadows and highlights to this location and camera direction. Preserve a coherent afternoon and color treatment across the series while allowing believable differences between sunlight and tree shade.

[ANATOMY AND PHOTOGRAPHIC REALISM]
Every person has exactly two arms, two hands, two legs and two feet. Each hand has five anatomically correct fingers including one thumb; fingers may be naturally occluded. Keep correct shoulders, elbows, wrists, joints, limb connections and left-right orientation. Respect the scene-specific hand assignments below. Do not add objects to empty hands, duplicate limbs, fuse fingers, merge the couple's arms or create impossible grips.
Render believable fabric folds, contact shadows, feet on the ground, body balance, matching illumination and lens perspective. Keep real skin texture. Do not use a pasted-face appearance, plastic skin, artificial HDR, extreme cinematic grading or exaggerated background blur.

[PHOTO 03/05 — A QUIET WISH IN FRONT OF THE MAIN SANCTUARY]
Use approved PHOTO 01 only for wardrobe, accessories and color continuity. Ground both faces in original real photographs, prioritizing the wife's real solo portrait with a gentle downward gaze. Move to the main sanctuary forecourt; do not copy an earlier shooting position.

LOCATION:
The paved forecourt directly in front of Som Rong's MAIN SANCTUARY, outside the building. Show recognizable Khmer columns and layered roof ornament as the main architectural background. Keep the photographed viewpoint grounded in a real sanctuary reference when available. Do not substitute the gray stupa or the reclining Buddha for the sanctuary.

ACTION AND EXPRESSION:
The husband stands on the image left and the wife on the image right, close enough to feel connected but with clearly separate bodies. They pause quietly and each brings their own palms together in front of their own chest. Eyes are gently lowered. Heads incline only a little; retain the original facial structure and enough visible facial detail for recognition. Use calm, sincere expressions, not exaggerated solemnity or a posed grin.

EXACT HAND PLACEMENT:
Each person has TWO hands joined in a simple natural prayer gesture at their own sternum, fingers pointing upward and thumbs close to the chest. Forearms rise from the correct elbows, wrists stay straight and relaxed, and shoulders remain lowered. The wife's hands must not become the husband's hands. Do not generate an additional arm, extra prayer hands, interlaced fingers or merged palms between the two people. Neither person holds incense, flowers or any other object.

CAMERA AND COMPOSITION:
Vertical 3:4, full-body portrait, both heads and shoes visible. Full-frame 50mm lens, f/5.6, nearly frontal camera around chest height with no dramatic upward or downward tilt. Keep both faces and both prayer gestures sharp. Give the sanctuary sufficient context without stretching the couple or bending the columns. Keep the bodies relaxed and feet naturally planted.

[FINAL OUTPUT CHECK]
Produce exactly ONE finished photograph, not a collage, storyboard or before-and-after comparison. Vertical 3:4 aspect ratio, high-resolution photographic detail, natural colors, no title text, captions, borders or watermark.
Before finalizing, compare both visible faces and hairstyles to the ORIGINAL REAL references, check the wife's unchanged full body proportions, check every visible hand and its assigned action, verify outfit continuity and confirm that this photograph shows its own specified physical location. If an expressive pose, beauty treatment or dramatic lighting would compromise identity or anatomy, preserve the original identity and anatomy first.
