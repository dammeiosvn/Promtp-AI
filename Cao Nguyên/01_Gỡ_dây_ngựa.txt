His head remains upright, chin level, with both eyes clearly visible. He looks close to the camera with a quiet, composed, confident expression and relaxed closed lips. FRAMING AND CAMERA

Include the couple from head to boots, the white horse and the tether post, with comfortable space around the group. Keep their faces large and clear enough to establish likeness. Keep the camp and snowy ridge readable in the background without letting them compete with the faces. The scene should feel like a real preparation moment before a shared ride, elegant and calm.

Use the supplied ORIGINAL, unedited photographs of the real husband and wife as the absolute references for their identities, natural physiques, skin tones, relative height and proportions. ANATOMY AND PHOTOGRAPHIC REALISM
Exactly two adults, one husband and one wife, with no duplicate people or extra riders. Each person has exactly two arms, two hands, two legs and two feet. Keep wrists, elbows, shoulders, hips, knees and ankles correctly connected. Hidden limbs must be explained by the pose and clothing. Preserve left-right orientation. No extra, fused, missing or twisted limbs; no floating hands, merged bodies, malformed grips or disconnected tack.
Create a believable professional photograph with natural perspective, detailed textiles, real horse hair, grounded contact shadows and restrained cinematic color. Avoid doll-like skin, plastic faces, generic fashion-model features, extreme body retouching, fantasy armor, cartoon rendering, artificial HDR, illegible text, logos and watermarks.
